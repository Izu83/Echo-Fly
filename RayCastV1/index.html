Echo RoomECHO-FLY PROTOTYPE

3D laser sonar · walls invisible · mouse to look · E or click to scan (hold to keep scanning) · Space/Shift fly

Click the view to look around with the mouse (Esc gives the mouse back). E or left-click fires a burst of lasers all around you; hold to keep scanning. Every hit lights up briefly, coloured by what it hit, then leaves a fading afterglow. Tab opens a big map.

Drag on the view to look around, use the left stick to walk, and hold SCAN to fire the lasers.

Tip: turn your phone sideways for a full-screen view.
